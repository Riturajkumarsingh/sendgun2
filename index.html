Understanding the Core

SMS, Bulk SMS & SMS API What You Need to Know

Sendgun brings together every flavour of SMS communication under one powerful platform.

SMS

SMS (Short Message Service) is the standard text message sent from one mobile device to another. It’s the most direct, reliable and universally accessible communication channel works on every phone, no internet required. With Sendgun, every SMS is delivered instantly via direct operator connections, ensuring the highest delivery rates across India.

Perfect for transactional alerts, OTPs, and personal messages

Bulk SMS

Bulk SMS refers to sending a large volume of SMS messages to many recipients at once typically used for marketing campaigns, announcements, reminders or mass alerts. With SendGun’s Bulk SMS dashboard, you can upload a CSV list, schedule campaigns, segment audiences and track performance in real time.

Ideal for promotions, offers, event notifications & emergency alerts

SMS API

SMS API (Application Programming Interface) allows developers to integrate SMS capabilities directly into their software, websites or mobile apps.
